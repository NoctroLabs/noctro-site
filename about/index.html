About Noctro Labs

We build the apps we wished existed.

The apps you use every day should be made with as much care as the things you love. We got tired of waiting for someone else to make them, so we started making them ourselves.

01 · How it started

It started at home. The best ideas usually do.

Kieran, our founder, collects trading cards and has a busy family to keep on track. Both deserved a great app. Every one he tried did half the job, and most still wanted paying every month for it.

There was no app that showed whether a collection had made money or lost it. There was no single place to see who's where, which chores are done, and what pocket money is owed. So instead of settling, he started building.

For the collectionSlabdEvery card, what it cost and what it's worth now.
For the familyNestwiseCalendar, chores, pocket money and chat, all in one place.

02 · Real life

We design for the moments that matter. Not the app store charts.

The card you've been chasing for months, finally in your hands. The morning rush to work out who's on the school run. A child's pride as they watch their pocket money add up.

These are the moments we build for. Every screen has to earn its place in someone's day: it should be quick, clear, and out of the way when you don't need it.

03 · Subscriptions

We're fed up of subscriptions too. So ours have to earn their place.

We've all been there: another monthly charge for an app that never seems to get any better. We refuse to make that app. Good apps do take ongoing work, so some of ours have a paid plan, and in return we promise you this.

These promises aren't buried in the small print. We build our apps around them. Slabd keeps every one of them today, and every app we launch will too.

A free version worth having

Not a trial, and not a teaser. Slabd's free plan holds a real collection of up to 75 cards, and you never need to enter card details.

Your data is always yours

Exporting your data is never behind a paywall.

Nothing held hostage

Stop paying, and everything you've added is still there.

Early believers are looked after

The people who back an app early help shape it, and they should be rewarded for it. Slabd's founding members get Premium free, permanently.

No tricks, ever

No fake countdowns, no guilt-trip pop-ups, and no maze when you want to cancel. You'll always see the price clearly before you pay.

Slabd's paid plan hasn't launched yet. Its pricing page shows what we intend to charge.

04 · How we work

A small team that builds with AI.

We're small on purpose. There are no layers of management and no committees watering ideas down, just people who care deeply, making every decision themselves.

And we build with AI at our side: designing, writing code, testing and checking each other's work. It lets a handful of people achieve what used to need a whole team. Every hour it saves goes into the details you'll feel.

AI does the heavy lifting

It builds, tests and reviews around the clock, so a good idea becomes a working app quickly.

People make the calls

Every design decision and every release needs a person to say yes. Nothing ships until it feels right.

So you get more, sooner

It's how a small studio can keep launching new apps all through 2027 without cutting a single corner.

05 · Perfection

Obsessed with the last 1%. Because that's the part you feel.

Plenty of apps are 99% finished. The last 1% is the difference between an app you put up with and one you love: the animation that lands just right, the message that actually helps, the sync that simply works.

We'll happily ship a week late rather than ship something we're not proud of. You'll never notice most of what we sweat over, and that's exactly the point.

  • Built natively for iPhoneIn Swift, following Apple's design guidelines, rather than a website squeezed into an app.
  • Tested on real phones, by real familiesNot just in a simulator. Our own households use every app before you do.
  • Private by defaultWe collect only what an app needs to work. No ads, and we never sell data.
  • Always improvingLaunch is the start. We keep improving our apps long after release.
