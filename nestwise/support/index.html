Common questions

How do I invite my family?

In Settings → Family sharing, add each person as a parent or a child, then send them their invite. On their iPhone, they install Nestwise, tap I've been invited to a family, and enter the invite code.

My child doesn't have a phone. Can they still use Nestwise?

Yes. Add them as a child. Parents can tick off their chores and keep track of their pocket money for them. If they get an iPhone later, send them an invite and their profile moves onto their own phone.

How does pocket money work?

Each child can earn money from chores, get a set weekly amount, or both. Money adds up through the calendar month, then it's payday. Parents can add a bonus, take money away, or mark a month as paid. A month that hasn't been paid stays as its own statement, so nothing gets lost.

Why does a chore say "Needs checking"?

When a child marks a chore as done, a parent checks it before the money is added. Parents will find these waiting under Needs checking and can approve them one at a time or all at once.

How does location sharing work?

Each person chooses whether to share their location, and it's off until they turn it on. To get a notification when someone arrives at or leaves home or school, allow location access Always in iPhone Settings. Only each person's latest location is kept. See the privacy policy for details.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for Nestwise in iPhone Settings → Notifications, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. In Nestwise, open Settings → Notifications and make sure they're turned on for this iPhone.

Can I bring in my work calendar?

Yes. In the Calendar tab, tap + then Import from Calendar, and choose which iPhone calendars to bring in, so trips and shifts appear for the family. Running the import again updates any events you imported before.

Can Nestwise limit my child's screen time?

Nestwise doesn't control Screen Time itself. Apple's own Screen Time does this best. Open Settings → Screen Time tips in Nestwise for a quick guide to setting it up.

How do I leave or delete a family?

In Settings, anyone except the person who created the family can leave it. The person who created it can delete the family, which permanently removes everything in it for everyone. To delete your account, email nestwise@noctro.co.uk.
