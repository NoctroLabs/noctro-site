Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Nestwise holds a lot about your family: plans, messages, money and, if you choose, where everyone is. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, where it's kept and how to remove it.

The short version

  • Your family's information is shared only with the people in your family.
  • We don't show ads, sell data, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Location sharing is off until each person turns it on, and only their latest position is kept.
  • Your family's data is stored with Google Cloud in London.
  • You can delete your family, and everything in it, whenever you like.

Who we are

Nestwise is made by Noctro Labs, an independent app studio in the UK. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can contact us about anything on this page at nestwise@noctro.co.uk.

What we collect, and why

Your account

You sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an identifier for your account and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see a private relay address. We use this to sign you in and connect you to your family.

Your family's content

Everything you and your family add to Nestwise is stored so it can sync between your devices and appear for the rest of your family. This includes:

  • names, roles (parent or child) and profile colours for each family member
  • calendar events, such as trips, shifts, school holidays and appointments
  • chores, who completed them, and whether a parent approved them
  • pocket money amounts, bonuses, deductions and payment records
  • chat messages, along with any photos, files or GIFs you send
  • places you save, such as home and school

Children who don't have their own device can be added as a profile managed by a parent. In that case, Nestwise holds only what the parent enters.

Location (optional)

Each family member decides whether to share their location. If they do, Nestwise stores their latest position, the time it was updated, their phone's battery level, and whether they are at one of your saved places. This lets the family map show where everyone is and send a notification when someone arrives at or leaves a place like home or school. We don't keep a history of past locations: each update replaces the one before it. If you allow location access "Always", updates can also happen while the app is in the background. You can stop sharing at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Calendar import (optional)

If you choose to import from your iPhone's calendars, Nestwise reads the calendars you select and adds their events to your family calendar. It doesn't read calendars you haven't selected, and it never changes your iPhone's calendars.

Notifications

To send notifications, such as a new message, a chore waiting for approval, or someone arriving home, we store a push token for each of your devices. A push token is an identifier Apple gives your device so notifications can reach it.

Face ID

You can lock Nestwise with Face ID and require it for money actions. Face ID is handled entirely by your iPhone. Nestwise never receives your face data.

GIF search

When you search for a GIF in chat, your search words are sent to GIPHY to find matching results. GIPHY's handling of that search is covered by GIPHY's privacy policy. No other Nestwise data is sent to GIPHY.

What we don't collect

Nestwise contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it to build marketing profiles.

Who can see your information

Your family's content can be seen only by members of your family who have joined with their own account, and what each person can do depends on their role. For example, only parents can approve chores or pay out pocket money. Invitations work through a one-time link that a parent shares.

We use these service providers to run Nestwise. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud): sign-in, database, file storage, server functions and notification delivery. Family data is stored in Google Cloud's London region (europe-west2).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and delivering notifications to your devices.
  • GIPHY: GIF search, as described above.

We will only disclose information if the law requires us to, for example in response to a valid court order.

Our legal basis

Under UK data protection law, we process your family's information because it's needed to provide the app you've asked to use. Optional features such as location sharing and calendar import rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by turning the feature off.

Children

Nestwise is designed for families, so it holds information about children. A parent creates the family and controls who joins it. Children's information is visible only to their own family and is never used for advertising or profiling. Location sharing for a child is off unless it's turned on on their device. Parents can edit or remove a child's profile and its content at any time.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your family's information for as long as your family exists in Nestwise.

  • Leaving a family removes your account's access to it and stops your devices receiving its notifications. Messages you sent and records you created stay with the family.
  • Deleting a family, which the parent who created it can do in Settings, permanently deletes the family and everything in it for everyone, including messages, photos and files.
  • Deleting your account: email nestwise@noctro.co.uk from the address linked to your Apple ID, or tell us which family you belong to, and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Deleting the app from your phone also removes the copy of your data stored on that device.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Server-side rules make sure each person can read and change only their own family's data. Optional Face ID adds a lock on the device itself.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To do any of these, email nestwise@noctro.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Nestwise uses your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let you know before it takes effect.
